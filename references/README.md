## 🎯 Purpose

This folder is the designated central repository for storing and maintaining **exact official layout references** for all ISO-standard documents generated by the system:

1. **Class Schedule** (ISO 9001:2015 Document Code: `INS-CLS-08`)
2. **Faculty Workload** (ISO 9001:2015 Document Code: `INS F22`)
3. **Room Utilization** (Institutional ISO Format)

You can save your institution's official scanned documents, blank Word templates (`.docx`), PDF sample outputs, and photographic/screenshot references in the designated subfolders below to ensure 100% compliance during ISO quality audits.

## 🖨️ Export Modes Supported in `ExportOptions.jsx`

For each of the three document types, the system supports three output channels:

### 1. Print Document (`print/`)
- **Trigger**: Browser native print dialog (`iframe.contentWindow.print()`).
- **Paper Standard**: US Letter (`8.5 in × 11 in`).
- **Margins**:
  - Landscape (Class Schedule): `0.25in top/bottom, 0.35in left/right`.
  - Portrait (Faculty Workload / Room Utilization): `0.2in - 0.25in`.
- **Page Rules**: `@page` rule enforcing `page-break-inside: avoid` and `-webkit-print-color-adjust: exact` so colors, backgrounds, and borders print cleanly without spilling onto page 2.

### 2. Save Image (`save-image/`)
- **Trigger**: High-resolution `html2canvas` render (scale `3x`, 300+ DPI equivalent).
- **Target Container Width**: `1100px` fixed snapshot envelope.
- **Output**: Lossless `.png` image ready for digital distribution, social channels, or mobile viewing.

### 3. Word Document (`word-docs/`)
- **Trigger**: Standard Microsoft Word HTML+VML/MIME envelope (`application/msword;charset=utf-8`).
- **Compatibility**: Microsoft Word 2007 through 2024, Office 365, LibreOffice Writer, and Google Docs.
- **Geometry**: Strict point-based fixed geometry (`pt`) preventing column stretching, cell shifting, or pagination breaks.

## 📥 How to Add Your Reference Files

When you receive official sample copies, scans, or templates from your administration:

1. **Scanned physical copies or PDFs**:
   - Save into `references/<document-type>/print/`
   - *Example*: `references/class-schedule-iso/print/Official_Signed_INS_CLS_08.pdf`

2. **Official Word templates or forms**:
   - Save into `references/<document-type>/word-docs/`
   - *Example*: `references/faculty-workload-iso/word-docs/INS_F22_Official_Blank_Template.docx`

3. **Screenshots or approved layout mockups**:
   - Save into `references/<document-type>/save-image/`
   - *Example*: `references/room-utilization-iso/save-image/Approved_Room_Layout_2026.png`
